Trust · guardrails, not vibes

What Uzam refuses to do.

The rules are in code, not in prompts — so they hold no matter which agent calls.

Never invents

Unknown symbols return the supported list. Missing data returns missing[], never a plausible fake. No contract is ever hardcoded.

Claim vs fact

Issuer statements are labeled CLAIM until independently verified. Grades cap at MEDIUM on issuer word alone — HIGH needs multiple sources plus tokenlist confirmation.

Unknowns stated

Every report ends with what couldn't be proven and what would change the conclusion. "Custodian UNKNOWN" beats a confident lie.

Never "safe"

Nine fixed risk categories, ranked high → low. The word "safe" appears nowhere in the codebase — only observed severity.
